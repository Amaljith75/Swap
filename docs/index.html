Trade things you have for things you want.

Swap is an Android app for swapping items with people nearby. No money involved. For people aged 18+.

  • List things you no longer use, with photos.
  • Offer your items for things near you. The owner can accept, decline or ask for something else.
  • Chat in the app, meet in a public place, swap, and rate each other.

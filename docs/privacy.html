Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective 5 November 2026

Swap is an app for trading items with people nearby, without money. This policy explains what data the Swap app and these web pages collect, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. Swap is run by an independent developer in India ("we", "us"). You can reach us at .

The short version
  • We collect what Swap needs to work: your email, your profile, your items, your offers and chats, and an approximate location for each item.
  • Item locations are rounded to a grid of about 500 m before they are saved. Other people only ever see a rounded distance such as "1.5 km away", never your address.
  • No ads, no selling or sharing your data for advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, in the app or on the delete account page.
  • Swap is only for people aged 18 or over.

Chats block phone numbers and links, so please don't try to share personal contact details there.

2. Who can see your data

  • Other Swap users see your display name, photo, bio, area, rating, number of swaps, your available items, and a rounded distance to them. Only the two people in an offer see its messages.
  • Shared item links. When someone shares one of your items, anyone with the link can see that item's title, first photo, category, condition and what you're looking for. The link page never shows who listed it or where.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • We can access data when needed to run the service, answer your requests, review reports, or meet a legal obligation.

3. Service providers

We use these companies to run Swap. They process data only on our instructions to provide their service:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, photo storage, live chat). Stores everything listed in section 1.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering push notifications). Receives your push token and the text of each notification.
  • Google Maps Platform (the map you see when choosing an item's area). Google receives the map area being shown, your IP address and device information, under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub (hosts these web pages and the downloadable Android app). GitHub may log your IP address when you visit.

These providers may store data on servers outside India. We do not sell your personal data or share it with anyone for advertising.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid order from Indian authorities, or to protect someone's safety.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, items and push tokens: until you delete them or your account.
  • Photos you remove or replace are deleted from storage, normally within a day.
  • Messages, offers and ratings are part of the other person's trade history too. When you delete your account they stay for the other person, but your name and photo are removed and you appear as "Deleted user".
  • Reports are kept for up to 2 years for safety and to handle repeat abuse.
  • Error reports: 90 days.
  • Our providers keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses in server logs) under their own retention rules.

5. Deleting your account

In the app go to Profile → Delete account, or use the delete account page in any browser. Deletion is immediate and permanent:

  • Deleted: your sign-in (email and password), profile details and photo, all your photos, items that nobody made an offer on, push tokens, and your block list.
  • Closed: any open offers and accepted swaps. The other person is told.
  • Kept without your name: messages, offers and ratings that form part of another person's trade history, and items that were part of an offer (title and category only, without photos or description).

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. You can edit your profile and items in the app at any time. For anything else, contact . We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The database only lets each person read what they are allowed to see, and every change goes through server-side checks. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use elsewhere.

8. Age limit

Swap is only for people aged 18 or over, because trades involve meeting people you don't know. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using Swap, contact us or report them in the app and we will remove the account.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the version and date above and ask you to agree again in the app.

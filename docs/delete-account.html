Delete your Swap account

You can also do this in the app: Profile → Delete account.

What happens
  • Deleted straight away: your sign-in (email and password), profile, photos, items nobody offered on, notification tokens and block list.
  • Open offers and accepted swaps are cancelled, and the other person is told.
  • Messages, offers and ratings that are part of someone else's trade history stay for them, shown as "Deleted user".
  • This can't be undone.
